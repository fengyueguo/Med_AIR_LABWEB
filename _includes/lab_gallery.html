Publicity

CUHK research team has established an AI surgical assistance platform for ESD, AI-Endo.

Artificial intelligence (AI) technology has rapidly been deployed in endoscopic diagnosis and treatment. CUHK’s Faculty of Medicine has recently completed two studies on AI-assisted endoscopic technology, proving its potential to enhance performance in GI cancer diagnosis and assist doctor training in endoscopy. Results were published in Nature Communications and Clinical Gastroenterology and Hepatology.
