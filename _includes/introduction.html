Short Bio:
Qi Dou is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK), with a courtesy appointment at the T Stone Robotics Institute. Her research focuses on the interdisciplinary field of medical image analysis, machine learning, and robotic surgery intelligence. She aims to advance innovative intelligent systems for enhanced medical diagnosis, intervention, and education through next-generation healthcare technology. Her notable works include 3D deep learning for medical image computing, model generalizability, federated learning on heterogeneous medical data, surgical video analysis, and SurRoL. Recent research interests include embodied AI for elderly care robotics, surgical robotics, LLMs for medical education, agentic AI systems for healthcare, and smart XR for medical education and surgery assistance.
